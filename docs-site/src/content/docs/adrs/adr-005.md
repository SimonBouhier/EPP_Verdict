**Date**: 2026-02-08

## Context
A single threshold on `consensus_score` does not capture robustness. A score of 0.8 with 2 models from the same family ≠ 0.8 with 3 architecture families.

## Decision
`derive_confidence_tier()` (`attestation.py:216`) uses: `consensus_score` + `models_consulted` + `architecture_families`. Hardcoded thresholds: verified ≥ 0.85, validated ≥ 0.70, proposition ≥ 0.40.

## Consequences
Do NOT revert to a single-threshold tier. The multi-criteria thresholds are the very reason the 5D signature exists.
